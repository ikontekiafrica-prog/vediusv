Client Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from prospective clients about workflow, communication, and delivery.

What is your typical project process?

1. Understand the need: Initial discovery call to learn your goals, target travelers/clients, and requirements.
2. Plan the solution: Structure the sitemap, design direction, and technical architecture.
3. Build with purpose: Develop the site with clean responsive code, SEO metadata, and performance optimization.
4. Review & refine: Test thoroughly on desktop and mobile viewports with your feedback.
5. Launch & support: Deploy to your domain, set up Google Search Console, and guide your team on usage.

Do you work with clients outside Tanzania?

Yes. While based in Arusha, Tanzania, I collaborate smoothly with international clients via WhatsApp, Google Meet, email, and clear asynchronous project updates.

Do you handle domain registration and hosting?

I help clients select and configure the best hosting environment, custom domains (e.g. .co.tz or .com), SSL certificates, and professional business emails so the client always maintains full ownership of their assets.

Ikon Safaris: Curated Tanzania Experiences

https://ikonsafaris.com/
View Live Site

1. Business Context & Objectives

Ikon Safaris offers private Tanzania safari, trekking, cultural, and coastal journeys shaped by local expertise, considered comfort, and meaningful experiences. Their primary customer base consists of international travelers from Europe and North America seeking curated, bespoke safaris to the Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Lake Manyara, and Lake Eyasi with the Hadzabe bushmen.

2. The Digital Challenge

The company needed a clean, elevated digital presence that diverged from generic, cluttered safari templates. The platform had to convey tranquil luxury, highlight authentic local operators, display clear package options, and make trip planning intuitive on mobile devices.

3. My Specific Technical Contribution

As developer, I implemented the custom package presentation architecture, allowing users to browse authentic multi-day itineraries (e.g., 4 Days Hadzabe & Crater, 5 Days Northern Luxury, 9 Days Wildebeest Migration). I structured responsive cards, mobile navigation, inquiry forms, and on-page technical SEO.

Verified Live Itinerary: Serengeti, Ngorongoro & Bushmen Cultural Safaris

Technologies

Responsive HTML5/CSS, Vanilla JS, Modular Backend, WebP Image Compression, Schema.org Microdata

Key Deliverables

Custom Package Grid, Responsive Navigation, Itinerary Detail Modals, WhatsApp Quick Inquiry

SEO Focus

Keyword targeting: "Curated Tanzania Experiences", "Private Tanzania Safari", fast LCP under 1.5s

Academic Results Processing System (ERM)

Documented System in CV: https://erm.free.nf
Inquire on Education Systems

1. System Context & Purpose

Developed as my first major enterprise software project, the Academic Results Processing System is designed for secondary schools to simplify academic grade management. It eliminates error-prone manual calculations and streamlines communication between teachers, students, and parents.

2. The Administrative Challenge

Secondary schools frequently process hundreds of student exam papers across various subjects, requiring complex GPA calculations, position rankings, and individualized print-ready report cards under tight academic deadlines.

3. My Specific Technical Contribution

As developer, I architected the relational database schema, implemented automated grading and division calculation algorithms according to national curriculum standards, engineered dynamic PDF report card generation, and designed role-based access for teachers and administrators.

Technologies

Automated Grade Calculations, Individual Student Reports, Teacher Data Entry Forms

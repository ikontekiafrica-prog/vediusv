Why I Do What I Do

Technology Should Help Real People Every Day

Writing code is easy, but making something truly useful is what matters. To me, a good website is not about confusing technical words. It is about helping a local safari company get more guests, helping a school share grades without mistakes, and helping young people in Tanzania learn skills that earn them a living.

I love working directly with clients. I listen to what you want to achieve, explain things in simple language, and build websites that are fast, easy to use on any phone, and ready to bring in real customers.

When I'm not coding client websites, you'll find me in the classroom teaching students how to use computers, or working alongside my mentors at the Institute of Accountancy Arusha.

What Working With Me Looks Like

Simple Language, No Jargon:

We talk about your real business goals and customer needs, not confusing technical acronyms.

Mobile First & Super Fast:

Direct Personal Care:

You collaborate directly with me from the first idea to the live launch and beyond.
